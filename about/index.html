About

Mark Stepchin

I’m an independent iOS developer. I built Recomp because I wanted a simple way to see physique changes over months — photos next to weight and notes — without sending those pictures to a server or pretending an algorithm can read body fat from a selfie.

Recomp is a local-first physique check-in app. You import or take progress photos, line up the same poses, and scrub between dates. Everything stays on your iPhone. No account. No upload. No AI body-fat estimates. It’s free, and it’s iOS only — I built it for the phone I use.

If something is broken, unclear, or missing, email me.
