Line up your progress photos

I built Recomp because no app made it fast to take, align and compare progress photos. Two photos framed differently can hide real change or fake it. A phone held lower makes your legs look longer. A step closer to the mirror makes your shoulders look wider. So Recomp helps at both ends: lining up the photos you have, and taking the next set the same way.

Line up any photo right in Compare. Drag, pinch or rotate either photo until it matches the other, and it saves when you lift your finger. Rotation snaps straight when you’re within 3°. After each change, a Reset button shows for about 3 seconds and puts the photo back the way it was shot.

Your original is never changed. The alignment is saved with that photo, so it carries into every comparison on the Compare page and into your Logs thumbnails. Front, side and back each keep their own.

The Alignment grid gives you lines to match. Turn it on from the Compare toolbar to lay a 3×3 grid over both photos. It’s off by default.

The in-app camera takes your next set in order. It walks front, side and back, and fills only the poses you haven’t taken yet. Skip leaves a pose empty, and Done ends the set. Your photo library stays the default, and Recomp remembers whether you last used Camera or Photos. A reminder tap opens Add a Log with that choice.

A self-timer lets you step back from the phone. It cycles Off, 3, 5 and 10 seconds and remembers your choice. Pinch to zoom, and flip between the rear camera, the default, and the front. Front photos save unmirrored, so they face the same way as rear shots.

Camera shots stay in Recomp. They save to the app, not your Photos library, and leaving without saving discards them. The camera never asks for your microphone.

The better the photo, the less there is to fix. My setup for consistent shots is in how to take progress photos for a recomp. I’m also testing automatic alignment, and I wrote up how progress photo auto-alignment works, and where it fails.

Recomp never uploads your photos. It sends anonymous usage analytics and crash reports, with no account and no tracking.

If a photo won’t line up, email me at support@progressphotos.app.
