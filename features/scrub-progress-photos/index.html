Scrub through your progress photos by date

Quick answer

In Recomp's Compare screen, tap the date under the before or after photo, and a ruler with one tick per check-in slides up. Drag it, and that photo changes with every check-in you pass, with a haptic tick on each one. The other side stays put, so you always compare against a fixed point.

Why first vs last isn't enough

The comparison that answers your question is rarely your first photo against your latest. During a recomp, the scale can sit still for months while your body changes. What you want to know is whether this month looks better than the same weight last spring, or which week a cut started to show.

How scrubbing works

Tap the date and weight under either photo. A sheet slides up with a horizontal ruler, one tick per check-in, your weight chart behind the ticks and the date and weight in large type. Fling or drag it, and it snaps to each check-in as that side's photo follows your finger.

The sheet takes at most the bottom half of the screen, with no dimmed backdrop. Both photos stay in view above it the whole time. Between scrubs, you can still pan, zoom and rotate either photo without closing the sheet.

Each side scrubs on its own. Pin your before photo to the day you started, then scrub only the after side through the months since. On a flat scale, scrub the before side to an earlier check-in at the same weight and see what changed.

Feel every check-in

Every check-in you pass gives one haptic tick. The tick fires in step with your finger, not a beat behind it, so you can count check-ins without looking at the ruler.

Liked check-ins tick harder. Double tap a photo to like it, and while you scrub, those check-ins give a stronger tick, so your best weeks are easy to find by feel.

Where Compare starts, and what stays put

Compare opens on your earliest liked check-in against your latest liked one. With no liked check-ins, it opens on your first check-in against your latest, and the older photo always sits on the left.

Switching Front, Side or Back keeps the dates you picked. If one of those check-ins has no photo for that pose, the slot says so, for example "No side photo", instead of jumping to another date. Once you've picked two dates, align them on the fly.

FAQ

Is this a before and after slider?

No. Recomp shows two photos side by side, and scrubbing changes which dates you see.

Can I type in a date?

Not today. You find a date by scrubbing, and the readout shows where you are.

Does it work with photos I imported?

Yes. Bulk import brings in up to 500 photos at a time and groups them into check-ins, one per date. It skips dates that already have a check-in, and every imported check-in shows up on the ruler.

Does scrubbing stay smooth with a big library?

Mid-scrub, Compare shows small thumbnails and loads the sharp photo when you stop. More on the timeline page.

Does Recomp upload my photos?

No. Recomp never uploads your photos, and there's no account.
