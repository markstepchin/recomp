A progress photo timeline you can fling through

Quick answer

Recomp's timeline is a ruler you fling. One swipe can carry the photo through years of check-ins, and it changes at every check-in you pass, like a flip-book of your own body. To stay smooth, it shows a small thumbnail while you move and loads the full photo when you stop.

What one fling shows you

Watching every week go by shows change that two photos can't. When my coaching ended, I had 450 front, side and back photos. Two photos side by side show where you ended up. Flinging through every check-in shows the weeks in between, in order.

Tap the date under either photo to open the ruler, then fling it. It snaps to each check-in, and the photo above changes at every one you pass, while the other side stays where you left it. Switch to Side or Back and fling again to watch the same months from another angle. Picking the exact two dates to compare is on the scrubbing page.

Why a big library gets slow

A 12 MP iPhone photo takes about 49 MB of memory once it's decoded (4032 × 3024 pixels at 4 bytes each). Decoding dozens of those while your finger moves is how a photo timeline starts to stutter.

How it stays smooth

Every photo has a small thumbnail, a sharp display image and the original. While you fling, Compare shows the thumbnail, which is enough to see your shape. When you stop, the sharp display image takes its place. The thumbnail is the display image scaled down with the same framing, so nothing jumps when the sharp version lands.

Recomp loads the photos around each side before you reach them. It keeps thumbnails and display images ready for the 6 check-ins on either side of each photo you're comparing. When you start scrubbing, it also loads every thumbnail for that pose, so the far end of your timeline starts loading before you fling to it.

How each photo keeps its alignment and stays sharp is on the Compare page.

FAQ

How fast is it?

I haven't measured it on a real phone with a real library yet, so I don't quote a number.

Is there a limit on how many photos I can keep?

I haven't set a cap on total check-ins or photos. Before saving new photos, Recomp checks that your phone has enough storage for them.

Why does a photo look soft for a moment?

You're seeing the thumbnail while you move. The sharp version replaces it when you stop.

Does Recomp upload my photos to load them faster?

No. Everything loads from your phone, and Recomp never uploads your photos.
