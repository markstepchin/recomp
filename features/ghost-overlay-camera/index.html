A ghost overlay camera for progress photos

Quick answer

Recomp 3.6 adds an in-app camera for check-ins. It shows your most recent photo of the same pose as a see-through ghost over the live view. You match your stance and framing before you take the shot, and the camera walks you through Front, Side and Back.

Why line up at capture

Some differences can't be fixed after the shot. Pan, zoom and rotate can't undo a turned hip or a camera held lower. Today you align on the fly in Compare, and a photo taken from the same spot and stance needs less of it.

Your last photo, as a guide

The ghost is your most recent photo of the pose you're taking. A side photo shows your latest side photo as the ghost, and if you lined that photo up in Compare, the ghost uses the aligned version. If there's no earlier photo of that pose yet, there's no ghost.

A 0 to 100 slider sets how see-through it is. It starts at 50, the camera remembers your setting next time, and the slider shows only when there's a ghost to adjust.

Front, Side, Back, in order

The camera walks you through the poses you haven't filled yet. The header shows where you are, like "Front · 1 of 3". Taking the shot moves you to the next pose, Skip leaves a pose empty and moves on, and Done ends the walk.

Your photo library stays the default source for new check-ins. Choose Camera or Photos when you add a check-in, and Recomp remembers your last choice. A reminder tap opens whichever one you used last.

Rear or front camera

The rear camera is the default, and a button flips to the front camera. On the front camera, the ghost is mirrored so it lines up with the preview. The photo you save is never mirrored, so front and rear shots face the same way.

A self-timer so you can step back

The timer has three settings: Off, 3 seconds and 10 seconds. Tap the timer button to cycle through them, and the camera remembers your choice for next time. A large countdown runs on screen, and tapping the shutter cancels it.

↑ Back to top

Your shots stay in the app

Camera shots are saved into Recomp's own storage, not your Photos library, and Recomp never uploads them. Each shot is dated today unless you change the date, and the camera doesn't ask for microphone access.

FAQ

Is the ghost camera available now?

Not yet. It's coming in Recomp 3.6, and today you add photos from your library and align them in Compare.

Does the camera align my photos automatically?

No. The ghost is a guide for you to match, and Recomp has no auto-alignment. I explain why in why auto-alignment is hard to do well.

What's the best setup for the camera?

Use the same spot, height and light every time. My setup is in how to take progress photos for a recomp.
