Compare progress photos side by side

I built Compare because I couldn’t see my own progress. When my coaching ended, I had 450 front, side and back photos. iOS Photos stored them but had no side-by-side view, so I flicked between two photos and guessed. During a recomp the scale can sit still for months while your body changes. The photos are the only evidence.

Any two check-ins sit side by side, with the weight and date under each. Compare opens on your earliest liked check-in against your latest liked one, with the older photo on the left. With one like, it pairs that check-in with your latest, or with your first if the liked one is the latest. With no likes, it’s your first against your latest. Double tap a photo to like it.

Switch to Slider to wipe between the two. Both photos share one frame, and dragging across it reveals the before photo over the after.

Tap a date to scrub through your whole progress photo timeline. A ruler slides up with one tick per check-in and your weight chart behind it. Fling or drag, and it snaps to each check-in as that side’s photo changes. Each side moves on its own, so you can keep your start date and scroll only the other photo. When the scale hasn’t moved, pick an old check-in at the same weight and compare.

You can feel your best weeks. Every check-in you pass gives a haptic tick, and liked check-ins tick harder. You can find them without watching the ruler.

Front, Side and Back keep your dates. Switch pose and both dates stay put. If a check-in has no photo for that pose, the slot says “No side photo” instead of jumping to another date.

Line them up on the photo itself. Drag, pinch or rotate either photo until your shoulders and hips match. The alignment saves to that photo, so it stays lined up in every comparison. More on lining up photos, and on the in-app camera, is on the Align page. I wrote about where auto-alignment fails separately.

Share one before and after image. The share button makes one JPG of what’s on screen, with the date and weight under each photo. In Slider, it shares the wipe instead of two full photos. It has no watermark or logo, and it opens the iOS share sheet.

Start with the photos you already have. Until your second check-in, Compare offers Bulk import. Pick up to 500 photos, and Recomp groups them into check-ins by the date each was taken. A CSV with date and weight columns fills in your weights.

Recomp never uploads your photos. It sends anonymous usage analytics and crash reports, with no account and no tracking.

If a comparison doesn’t show what you need, email me at support@progressphotos.app.
