## Apple Photos: free, already on your iPhone

  • Free, built into iOS; iCloud beyond 5 GB is paid. [Sourced: roundup draft-v8]
  • The Hidden album keeps photos out of your grid, locked behind Face ID by default. A Shared Album can go to a coach. No side-by-side view. [Sourced: roundup draft-v8] [Sourced: research plan, Oct 4, US store page]
  • Weight lives in a note or spreadsheet, apart from the picture. After a year, finding "week 6" takes a lot of scrolling. [Sourced: roundup draft-v8]

## Google Photos: a two-photo collage built in

  • Builds a two-photo collage on iPhone (iOS 18+, app 7.64+, per Google's help center). [Sourced: research plan, Oct 4, US store page]
  • 15 GB of free Google storage, then Google One from $1.99 a month. [Sourced: research plan, Oct 4, US store page]

## Hevy: photos next to your lifts

  • One progress photo per day, square, with an overlay of your last photo at capture and a compare view. Photos are free and stored in your Hevy account. [Sourced: research plan, Oct 4, US store page]
  • Pro is $2.99 a month, $23.99 a year or $74.99 lifetime. Free keeps 3 months of history; whether that hides older photos is unverified [CONFIRM]. [Sourced: Steve's Hevy report]

## MacroFactor: photos inside your macro tracker

  • Front, side and back, one per pose per day, with crop, zoom and rotate and a before/after compare, per its release notes. [Sourced: research plan, Oct 4, US store page]
  • Subscription only: $11.99 a month, $47.99 for 6 months or $71.99 a year, after a 7-day trial. [Sourced: research plan, Oct 4, US store page]
  • Users say photos are hard to find: "Please for the love of god make progress photos more easily accessable." (US, 4 stars, Feb 28, 2026). [Sourced: Steve's MacroFactor report]

## MyFitnessPal: one photo per weigh-in

  • One photo per weigh-in, cropped square, stored on MyFitnessPal's servers, with no alignment. Photos are free; data export is Premium. [Sourced: research plan, Oct 4, US store page] [Sourced: Steve's MyFitnessPal report]
  • Premium is $19.99 a month or $79.99 a year. [Sourced: research plan, Oct 4, US store page]

## Collage and before/after makers: one image, not a record

  • PicCollage: the most-rated collage app (1.84M US ratings). Free with a watermark; $4.99 a month, $35.99 a year, or $1.99 to remove the watermark. [Sourced: research plan, Oct 4, US store page]
  • Layout: the listing names before and after layouts. Free; Pro $2.99–4.99, $29.99, or $39.99 for family. [Sourced: research plan, Oct 4, US store page]
  • Photo Compare (Devnex): side by side, slider and video; top 10 in App Store search for core progress-photo terms. Ads and a watermark on the free tier, with weekly plans up to $4.99. [Sourced: research plan, Oct 4, US store page]
  • None of them stores a date, weight or pose with the photo [CONFIRM per app]. [TODO: test each with the same before/after pair.]
