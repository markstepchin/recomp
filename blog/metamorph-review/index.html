Metamorph review: the priciest lifetime plan, and you place every photo by hand

Quick Answer

I don't recommend Metamorph. It's expensive for a general-purpose before/after app: $129.99 lifetime, the most of any app I graded. Its ghost overlay helps at capture, but it has no weight logging, and you place every compare photo by hand. [CONFIRM: provisional quick answer pending the full test]

Full disclosure: I build Recomp, a free progress photo app that competes with this one. No app pays me [CONFIRM: no ads, affiliate links or partner deals]. This review expands the Metamorph entry in my roundup of the best progress photo apps, scored with the same kit.

What it costs

A free 7-day trial, then $29.99 a year, $4.99 a month or $129.99 lifetime. The store also lists $4.99 a week. The trial arrived in 1.3 on Dec 12, 2024, and paying removes the watermark. [TODO: what the free tier allows after the trial]

Taking a check-in

I proposed 3 out of 5 for capture. It has a ghost overlay and a timer, but no weight logging. The timer arrived in 1.7 on Jan 23, 2025, and since 2.15 the overlay can be your first or your most recent photo. [TODO: seconds and taps to a saved front, side and back check-in]

Lining photos up

I proposed 0 for alignment: there's no alignment tool after the shot. The ghost helps you line up before it. Its own site describes "the automatic alignment overlay to match your position, angle, and zoom to your previous photo before you shoot." An alignment grid arrived in 2.17 in Aug 2026. Comparing two dates

I proposed 2 for comparison. It has a video time-lapse, a slider, 1x2 and 2x1, but the UI is poor, and you place each photo by hand. Picking a pair from hundreds of photos is slow in every app I tested, and this is the most manual version. [TODO: seconds to put the oldest and newest front photos side by side]

Importing old photos

Metamorph imports existing photos from your library. Its site doesn't say whether it reads each photo's date. [TODO: import time, date handling and scroll test with the full library]

Where your photos live

Face ID locks the app, and photos stay on your device or iCloud, per its listing. It doesn't explain whether iCloud means sync, a backup, or iCloud Photos as a source. Its paywall advertises cloud sync. [TODO: delete-and-restore test]

Where Metamorph came from

Metamorph began as a stop-motion app for renovations, plants and kids before it turned to body progress. Its own blog ranks Metamorph first. One 5-star review in my review study uses it for home renovations.
