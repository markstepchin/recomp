Body Tracker review: a one-time price, but alignment that doesn't save

By Mark Stepchin · Updated [CONFIRM: publish date]

Quick Answer

Body Tracker by Igor Korolev has a one-time Pro price and a decent camera, but its compare step got in my way. Pro is $7.99 once. It has manual alignment, but my adjustments didn't save. To change one photo in a comparison, I had to delete the compare view and pick both photos again. [CONFIRM: provisional quick answer]

Full disclosure: I build Recomp, a free progress photo app that competes with this one. No app pays me [CONFIRM: no ads, affiliate links or partner deals]. This review expands the Body Tracker entry in my roundup of the best progress photo apps, scored with the same kit.

The camera has front, side and back poses, two frames and guide lines. Frame switches between Waist up and Full body. Comparing two dates

Compare has Slider and Side by side, and it works on two photos at a time. To swap only one side, I had to delete the comparison and pick both photos again. With 20 dates to look through, that's 20 rebuilds instead of a scroll. [CONFIRM: "20 rebuilds" is an illustration; remove if Mark prefers only what he did]
