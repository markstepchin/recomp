**Alignment exists, but it didn't save for me.** You pinch and drag the photo, and sliders set scale, horizontal and vertical position. When I came back, the adjustment was gone. <mark class="rounded-sm bg-yellow-200 px-1 text-black box-decoration-clone">\[CONFIRM: steps that reproduce it, app version, and whether it's lost on leaving compare or on relaunch\]</mark>

## Comparing two dates {#comparing-two-dates}

**Compare has Slider and Side by side, and it works on two photos at a time.** To swap only one side, I had to delete the comparison and pick both photos again. With 20 dates to look through, that's 20 rebuilds instead of a scroll. <mark class="rounded-sm bg-yellow-200 px-1 text-black box-decoration-clone">\[CONFIRM: "20 rebuilds" is an illustration; remove if Mark prefers only what he did\]</mark>
