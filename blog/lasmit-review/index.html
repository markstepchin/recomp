Lasmit "Progress" (Body Measurements Tracker App) Review (2026)

[CONFIRM: publish date]

Lasmit's "Progress" has 9,355 ratings since 2012, the most of any progress photo app I tested, and it gave me a headache. It's a measurement tracker first, with two-way Apple Health sync. I can tell someone put a lot of effort into it, but everything feels awkward, and compare works only in landscape. [Sourced: Mark's hands-on notes, Oct 3, in draft-v8]
