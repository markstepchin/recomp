Fit-Stitch Progress Tracker Review (2026)

[CONFIRM: publish date]

Fit-Stitch is the only paid progress photo app I tested with no subscription: $4.99 once. It syncs to your own iCloud and logs a check-in on one clean form. Comparing is the weak spot, because every comparison is a card built in its own form. [Sourced: Mark's hands-on notes, Oct 3, in draft-v8]

I built Recomp, a free progress photo app that competes with this one. No app pays me [CONFIRM: no ads, affiliate links or partner deals].
