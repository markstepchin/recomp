Why I created Recomp

After years of weekly progress photos, I couldn’t find an app that made them fast to take, align, and compare. Recomp is a check-in that takes under a minute.

See the progress buried in your camera roll

Import your photos and weight in under a minute. Compare progress photos on a before-and-after timeline, scroll to any date instantly, and align photos on the fly. Your photos and data never leave your device.
