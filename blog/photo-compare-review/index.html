Photo Compare review: decent alignment for one before/after, with ads

By Mark Stepchin · Updated [CONFIRM: publish date]

Quick Answer

Photo Compare is a before/after image maker, and its alignment is decent. The free version compares two photos and shows ads. It doesn't keep a weekly record of check-ins, so it fits one before/after, not tracking. [CONFIRM: provisional quick answer; "doesn't keep a weekly record" needs Mark's check]
