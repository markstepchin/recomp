GainFrame: Gym Progress Photos Review (2026)

[CONFIRM: publish date]

GainFrame is the most polished progress photo app I tested, and with hundreds of photos it was pretty much unusable. The colors, icons, spacing and import loading states are the best in the category. But the UI puts AI first: photos sit in small boxes with AI labels over them, and my import stopped at 250 photos after about 15 minutes. [Sourced: Mark's hands-on notes, Oct 3, in draft-v8] [Sourced: Mark's notes, Oct 4]

## Where your photos live

  • Photos stay on your iPhone with no account needed, but AI features send them to Google's Gemini, which discards them, per its policy. The label includes "Data Used to Track You." Deleting the app removes your photos. Apple Health and Hevy connect. [Sourced: roundup draft-v8]
