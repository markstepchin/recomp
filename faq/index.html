FAQ

Common questions

Short version for now. A longer Recomp FAQ is on the blog backlog and will land here later.

Does Recomp upload my photos?

No. Photos and check-ins stay on your iPhone. See Privacy.

Is Recomp free?

Yes. iOS only.

Does it estimate body fat with AI?

No. Recomp is for photos, weight, and notes — not body-fat guesses.

How should I take progress photos?

Same place, lighting, and poses every 1–2 weeks. Full write-up: How to Take Progress Photos for a Recomp.
